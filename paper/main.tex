\section{模型的建立与求解}

\subsection{问题一模型}

建立如下优化模型：

\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:model}
  \begin{aligned}
    \min_{\bm{x}} \quad & f(\bm{x}) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i \left( x_i - \mu_i \right)^2 \\
    \text{s.t.} \quad   & \bm{A}\bm{x} \leq \bm{b}, \\
                        & x_i \geq 0, \quad i = 1, 2, \ldots, n.
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
